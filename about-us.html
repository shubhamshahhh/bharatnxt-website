Field note · An early market visit

“I can only use this card to buy from one person. What’s the point?”

A buyer of consumer durables

A buyer said this to us on an early market visit. We were still Finovate Capital then, focused on Supply Chain Finance. His words stayed with us.

Credit that works in only one place isn’t freedom, and it doesn’t solve a business owner’s real problem.

Over time, that question became BharatNXT.

01The turn 02Today 03Built to last 04Technology 05AI-first 06What’s next
01The turn

One card. One supplier. That was the problem.

Supply Chain Finance tied a buyer’s credit to a single supplier. But no business buys from just one. We set out to build credit that goes wherever the business needs it to.

03Built to last

Built to last, not just to grow.

We have always put the ecosystem first. Card issuers, issuing and acquiring banks, payment aggregators and our customers all have to win for any of it to work, so we protect the commercial interests of each of them.

We build profitable, sustainable businesses that customers can count on when they need them most.

Every transactionEveryone wins, or none do
Card issuers Issuing banks Acquiring banks Payment aggregators Our customers

That belief has shaped hard decisions.

The easy option Ship with no friction. Nothing requires it.

We added controls and deliberate friction anyway.Even when neither regulation nor commerce required it.

The easy option Take the quick gain in GTV.

We chose long-term sustainability.Over a quick gain in Gross Transaction Volume. We would make the same choice again.
