The app

Everything in one app.

Card payments, invoices, marketplace, collections, working capital and one ledger, on your phone. No integration, no IT team, no change of bank.

Card Payments

Pay any supplier, card machine or not

Pay on the card you already hold, even suppliers who have never accepted a card. They receive a regular bank transfer into their existing account. Full invoice amount, within 24 hours.

Invoice Payments

Every invoice you owe, in one place

Connect your Gmail, your GST account and NPCI Bharat Connect. Invoices arrive in the app on their own. Review, approve, pay. Nothing typed in by hand, nothing missed on a due date.

Marketplace

Collections
